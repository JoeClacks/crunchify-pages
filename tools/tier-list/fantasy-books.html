How to Use

Click any item below the unranked pool to add it to your list, or drag it directly into a tier. Use the sidebar to switch to a different template. Drag items into tiers to rank them, and click any item to view details, follow links, or move it to a different tier.

Your rankings are saved automatically. Use the list switcher at the top to manage multiple tier lists. Export as a PNG image, a shareable .tierlist file, or a CSV for importing into Goodreads and other sites.
