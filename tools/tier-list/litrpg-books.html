How to Use

Drag items from the unranked pool into a tier to rank them. Click any item to view details, follow referral links, or move it to a different tier. Use the Export button to download your tier list as a PNG image, a shareable .tierlist file, or a CSV for importing into Goodreads and other sites.

Your rankings are saved automatically. Use the Save button to store named snapshots and switch between different rankings.
