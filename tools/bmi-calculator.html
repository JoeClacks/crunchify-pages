How It Works

Body Mass Index (BMI) is calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared (kg/m²). For imperial units, the formula is (lbs / inches²) × 703. The WHO defines four categories: Underweight (< 18.5), Normal (18.5–24.9), Overweight (25–29.9), and Obese (≥ 30).

BMI is a useful screening tool but has well-known limitations — it doesn't distinguish between muscle and fat mass, varies by age and ethnicity, and can misclassify athletes as overweight. Consult a healthcare provider for a full health assessment.
